Oars and Sails v1.0.79 - Tune sheet
===================================

Open the console with ~ and type a command. Everything you change is saved in
Data\SKSE\Plugins\OarsAndSails.ini and kept between games.
Board the boat first: the numpad only works on your own boat.

1. Numpad modes (the numpad is off until you pick one)
   oas "num speed"    rowing feel
                        Num8 / Num2  faster / slower (steps of 10)
                        Num9 / Num3  longer / shorter glide between strokes
                        Num4 / Num6  turn slower / faster
                        Num0         reset speed, glide and turning
   oas "num height"   how high the boat sits in the water   Num9 / Num3, Num0 + Num9 reset
   oas "num floor"    plank floor height                    Num9 / Num3, Num0 reset
   oas "num ripple"   move a stern ripple                   Num5 switches left / right
   oas "num stow"     oars lying in the parked boat
   oas "num crew"     crew seats, one at a time            Num5 next seat
   oas "num dog"      dog seats, one at a time             Num5 next seat
   oas "num npc"      NPC lake boat height
   oas "num rower"    the rowing seat
   oas "num oars"     oars in the rower's hands
   oas "num off"      numpad off
   The keys for each mode also show on screen when you turn it on.

    Speed mode keys:   [7]      [8 faster]      [9 longer glide]
                       [4 turn slower] [5]   [6 turn faster]
                       [1]      [2 slower]      [3 shorter glide]
                       [0 reset all three   ]   [.]

2. Direct values                                          default
   oas "row speed 300"      top rowing speed               300
   oas "row drag 0.07"      glide (lower = longer glide)   0.07
   oas "row turn 0.45"      turning                        0.45
   oas "boat height 5"      hull height in the water       5
   oas "floor 3"            plank floor offset             3
   oas "eye 22"             first person view height       22   (-40 to 80, not saved yet)
   oas "ripple wake 0.05"   stern ripple size              0.05
   oas "ripple oar 0.05"    oar glide ripple size          0.05
   oas ripples              ripples on / off               on
   oas paintblue            boat colour: paintblue, paintbeige, paintblack, paintred,
                            paintwhite, paintbluedesign, paintvanilla
   oas "lakeboats 6"        NPC boats per lake (0-10), also "lakeboats off" / "lakeboats on"
   oas "reset settings"     everything above back to the defaults
   oas help / oas status    all commands / current values

3. Boat spots
   Your boat waits at the spots in OarsAndSails_Docks.ini. Walk within ~2500 of one and the boat is
   there, facing the way the spot was placed. A boat you left within ~3000 of you is never moved.
   oas "dock list"          all spots and how far away they are
   oas "dock add <name>"    save your boat's current spot (and facing) as a new spot
   oas "dock del"           remove the nearest spot
   oas "dock off" / "dock on"

4. Where the numbers sit (side view)

        eye 22 (first person view)
           o                                   boat height 5: whole hull up / down
          /|        ________________________  /
   stern ____|_____/                        \/ bow
         \  floor 3 (plank floor) ___________/
   ~ ~ ~ ~\~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~/~ ~ ~ ~  waterline
   ripples \______________________________/

   The floor rides with the hull, so set the boat height first, then the floor.

5. Ini-only settings (Data\SKSE\Plugins\OarsAndSails.ini, [Camera])
   FaceRower=1            third person camera faces you when you row alone (0 = stays behind you)
   FirstPersonFreeLook=1  first person stays seated and looks all around (0 = the game's seated look limit)
