RowRim (Oars and Sails) v1.7.40 - Tune sheet
===========================================

Open the console with ~ and type a command. Words after "oas" go in quotes when there is more than one:
  oas "row speed 300"      (the console runs unquoted words together; oas paintblue is fine as one word)
"rr" is a second name for the same command. Settings are saved in Data\SKSE\Plugins\RowRim.ini
(with MO2 the file lands in the overwrite folder) and kept between games. No MCM is needed: everything is here.

1. Your boat
   oas boat                 a boat on the water ahead of you (the old one is taken away)
   oas bring                the boat under your feet
   oas unboat               remove your boat
   Look at the boat and press E (gamepad: A):
     [E] Board Rowboat      on the hull from the water, under it or from the shore: you land standing in the boat
     [E] Row Oars           on the middle thwart: sit at the oars and row
     [E] Steer Helm         on the stern seat: sit at the stern and steer
   E while seated stands you up. Standing in the boat, E is the game's own (pick up what you dropped): only the
   Row Oars / Steer Helm prompts sit you down.
   (The prompts need OarsAndSails.esl ticked; without it E in the boat still works.)
     [E] Unroll Bedroll     on the bedroll slung under the bow thwart: lays it out in the boat to sleep in (it stays);
                            a lantern is lit by it, the oars are laid along the gunwales, the dog sits at the stern
     [E] Fish               on the Fishing Supplies, standing close (Creation Club Fishing): fish over the side you face
     [E] Pack Up Bedroll    on the same spot once it is out: rolls it back up under the thwart
   oas "bedroll roll" / "bedroll unroll" do the same from the console
   Sitting down rolls the bedroll up for the ride.
   [Clutter] BedrollScale=0.9, SuppliesScale=0.5   their sizes
   oas "num gear"           the numpad places the bedroll and the fishing supplies: Num5 picks which one,
                            8/2 forward/back, 4/6 left/right, 9/3 up/down, 7/1 turn; saved in RowRim.ini [Clutter]
                            (BedrollX/Y/Z/Yaw, SuppliesX/Y/Z/Yaw); oas "num off" stops it
   oas paintblue            colour: paintblue, paintbeige, paintblack, paintred, paintwhite, paintbluedesign, paintvanilla
   oas "height 0"           how high the hull sits in the water (-30..30, 0 = default)
   oas "lamp on|off"        the stern lantern (lit at night)

2. Rowing feel                                           default   ini (RowRim.ini [Row])
   oas "row speed 300"      top rowing speed              300       MaxSpeed=300
   oas "row drag 0.07"      glide (lower = longer glide)  0.07      Glide=0.070
   oas "row turn 0.45"      turning                       0.45      Turn=1.00 (a scale of 0.45)
   W rows, S backs water, A / D turn. Gamepad: left stick.
   An Oars and Sails install's OarsAndSails.ini [Row] is taken over once, the first time RowRim starts.

3. Boat spots (docks)
   Your boat waits at the spots in Data\SKSE\Plugins\RowRim_Docks.ini (13 ship with the mod: Riverwood, Lake Ilinalta (2),
   Half-Moon Mill, Riften, the Rift (2), Ivarstead, Darkwater Crossing, Windhelm, Morthal, Solitude, Dawnstar).
   Walk within ~7000 of one and the boat is waiting there, facing the way the spot was placed, in that spot's own colour.
   A boat you left within ~4000 of you is never moved.
   oas "dock list"          all spots, how far away, their colour
   oas "dock add <name>"    save your boat's current spot and facing as a new spot (get off first; with no boat
                            near you, one is placed ahead and that spot is saved)
   oas "dock del"           remove the nearest spot (within 2000)
   oas "dock off" / "dock on" / "dock reload"
   Workflow: oas boat (or row in and get off), line it up, then oas "dock add <name>".

4. Crew and passengers
   Followers board with you and sit on the thwarts (one dog in the bow). Weapons are sheathed and shields put away.
   When you get up they stay seated until you walk off: [Crew] StandUpAt=400 (about 6 m). Dismissed followers stay off.
   Sit at the stern with a follower aboard and one of them (picked at random, never the dog) takes the oars:
   W / S / A / D then row and steer through them. Sit at the oars yourself and they ride as passengers.
   oas "crew add"           click an NPC in the console first, then this: they ride with you (5 seats)
   oas "crew remove" / "crew clear" / "crew list" / "crew summon"
   oas num                  numpad seat tuning (Num5 picks who, 8/2 forward/back, 4/6 left/right, 9/3 up/down,
                            7/1 turn; saved per seat in [CrewSeats]); oas num again (or "num off") stops it,
                            oas "num reset" puts every seat back

5. NPC lake boats
   oas "lakeboats 3"        boats per lake (0-10), default 3      [LakeBoats] Count=3
   oas "lakeboats on|off"                                         [LakeBoats] On=1
   oas "lakeboats view 5"   how many are drawn at once (1-10)     [LakeBoats] View=5
   oas lakes                the lakes known around you

6. Plankton (dusk and night) and the water
   oas "plankton on|off"    on / off                               [Plankton] On=1
   oas "plankton night"     always (to look at it by day), "plankton auto" back by the time of day
   oas "plankton density 3" (0-6)   "plankton size 0.5"   "plankton open 1800" (0-2000)   "plankton shore 400" (0-400)
   Plankton rides the current: in rivers and rapids it drifts downstream with the water.
   oas "underwater on|off"  RowRim's own clear underwater look for the camera   [Underwater] Filter=1
                            how far you see under water: [Underwater] FogNear=150, FogFar=2100
   [Wake] On=1              the stern trail on the water
   oas "num stern"         the tiller's ripples: Num8/2 aft/forward, 4/6 closer/further apart, 9/3 up/down,
                            7/1 smaller/bigger (0 = off); [Wake] SternY/SternSpread/SternZ/SternSize
   oas "num hull"          the hull's ripples: 7/1 smaller/bigger (0 = off), 4/6 closer to/further from the hull,
                            8/2 fewer/more often, 9/3 up/down, Num-/Num+ fewer/more spots a side, Num0 stem on/off,
                            Num. at rest on/off; [Wake] HullSize/HullOut/HullEvery/HullZ/HullCount/HullStem/HullRest

7. Controller
   A (the button mapped to Activate)   sit / stand up, also while seated
   Left stick                          row and turn
   Hold R3 + left stick up/down        zoom the third person camera while seated (no rowing meanwhile)
   Hold L3 + right stick up/down       the same
   [Controls] PadZoom=1 (0 = off), PadZoomSpeed=1.2

8. Other
   oas help                 the player commands     oas status   current values     oas dev   test commands
   oas "reset settings"     row speed, glide, turning, height, paint, docks on, 3 lake boats back to the defaults
   [Clutter] Supplies=1     the usable Fishing Supplies in the boat (needs Creation Club Fishing, ccbgssse001-fish.esm)
   [Sound] OarDigSound=<form id, hex> (0 = off), OarDigVolume=0.8
