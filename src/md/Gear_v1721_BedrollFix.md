# v1.7.21: bedroll fixes

1. Laid itself out again: v1.7.12 stowed the bedroll while you sit and put it back the moment you stood (also on the way out of the boat), as [Clutter] Bedroll=1 stayed on. Now sitting down rolls it up for good (Bedroll=0, HUD "Bedroll rolled up for the ride"); E on the roll ("Unroll Bedroll") lays it out again.
2. Stray beds over the water after a load: the saved pieces the co-save lists were only removed inside Gear::Tend, which runs only once a boat is placed; with no boat out (or before it came in) they stayed. Now Gear::TendLoad runs every player update, boat or not: the listed pieces go at once, and for 30 s after a load (every 3 s, as cells come in) any bedroll / fishing supplies ref of ours (FF, our base, not the one held) lying within 200 of the water surface or within 600 of our hull is removed. Log: "Gear: stray Bedroll ... removed (over the water | by the boat)".
3. Fishing Supplies never showed: the log said "ccbgssse001-fish.esm is loaded but has no object 0x8D0", yet the record is in the file (read from the esm: 0x8D0 ccBGSSSE001_FishingFurniture01_Arctic, plus 0x806 / 0x8A6 / 0x89F / 0x8D1, all CreationClub\BGSSSE001\Clutter\FishingGear_Cluster01.nif). TESDataHandler::LookupForm by file name came back empty. Now the form ID is built from the file's own load index (light or full), trying 0x8D0 then the other variants, then any activator of that file with the FishingGear_Cluster01 model. Log: "Gear: fishing supplies XXXXXXXX from ccBGSSSE001-Fish.esm (index NN)" or "... found by its model".
4. Bedroll placed and removed every frame (log 07:02:08: dozens a second, "left in another cell"): the check was "filed in another cell than the hull ref"; near a cell edge the bed (a little ahead / behind the hull) is filed in the next cell while the hull ref is not yet (it hops every 0.5 s), so it was removed and placed again each frame; deleted beds that did not stick are the strays over the water. Now it is only placed again if its cell has unloaded or it is over 400 from its spot on the boat, and never in the same frame (0.5 s wait).

NB the 07:00 log was built before the fishing supplies change (it still shows the old "has no object 0x8D0" line).

## Crew (same build)
5. Dog thrown when the boat stops / you get up: on standing up everyone was let go at once: collision back on while the dog's body overlapped the hull's collision (put down at deck + 4), and the physics threw it out. Now, when you stand up, the passengers and the dog stay seated (held on their seats on the boat, drawn on it) until the boat has settled: under 25 speed for 1.2 s (8 s at most). Then they stand: put down with no speed first (the dog 14 above the floor instead of 4), their collision on after that. Sitting down again meanwhile keeps them seated. Log: "Crew: you stood up, N stay seated until the boat settles" / "Crew: the boat settled after X s, they stand up".

## Also in v1.7.21
6. The 07:12 build had an older Gear.inl and Plugin.cpp than intended (the files were re-sent from the same staged path and the old copy went over): no cell fix, and the passengers' wait was never ticked (Crew::Linger not called), so Meeko was left seated and not held on his seat. Both are in now.
7. Fishing supplies fading at cell edges: crossing into the next cell reloads the ref's 3D, which fades in from nothing; held fully drawn in Gear::LatePin (as the hull).
8. Soft collision (UpdateHullFeel / FeelReact): any object ahead of the bow (layer static, anim static, props, clutter, trees, not ours, not ice: jetties, docks, longboats, wood, boulders) is met with a soft stop: the bow feelers look ahead half the stop's travel (speed x 0.8 / 2), the stop is eased in and out over the time to cover the gap (0.25-0.8 s), with a lighter knock (0.015 rad at 300). Bank boulders (steep terrain at the waterline, OAS 48.2) stop the same way. A small rock (model with "rock", top under 40 over the water) only scrapes and bumps the keel. Sides and stern still scrape and push off, keel bumps as before. Was: a 0.2 s stop only over 150 closing speed, a 4% scrape below (slow boats slid into jetties). Log: "Feel: soft stop bow ... over N s".
